Build Along Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Build Along is a brick-building game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. In short: the app has no accounts, collects no personal information, and keeps your game on your device. Information leaves your device only to show ads, to process purchases you choose to make, and to back up your game if your device's backup is turned on.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your game: the models you own and built, your coins, boxes and progress.
  • Your settings: sound, music and game switches.

We don't run a server that receives any of this. Uninstalling the app deletes it from your device.

Backup

On Android, if Google backup is turned on for your device, Android copies the app's save file to your Google account so your game comes back when you reinstall or move to a new phone. On iOS, your device backup (iCloud or computer) may include it the same way. These backups are handled by Google or Apple under your account and their policies. We cannot see them.

Building instructions

The app may download new models and building instructions from our content server. Like any web request, this reveals your device's IP address to the server for delivery. Nothing that identifies you is sent and we don't keep these addresses for any other purpose.

Ads

Build Along is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a Google Play certified ad network. Because the game can be played by children, every ad request is marked as directed to children: ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, are never personalized, and are not based on the player's past activity. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect from the device:

  • the IP address and general location (country or city level),
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language),
  • how ads are seen and tapped, and crash information about ads.

The app does not ask to track you across other apps. Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the EEA and UK the app shows Google's consent form where the law requires it, and you can change your choice any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Purchases

You can buy coin packs inside the app. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, email or card details. To confirm purchases and restore them, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous app ID, the purchase receipt (product, price, date, transaction ID), and basic device and app information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Who information is shared with

  • Google (AdMob): to show child-directed ads, as described above.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: to process payments.
  • RevenueCat: to confirm and restore purchases.

We do not sell information, and we share nothing else with anyone.

Children

Build Along is designed for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. The app has no chat, no accounts and no way to share content with other players. If you believe a child's personal information reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load ads, building instructions, and to process purchases.
  • Billing: to let you buy coin packs through Google Play.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location.

Your choices

  • Change ad privacy choices in Settings (EEA/UK).
  • Reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Delete everything the app stored by uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
